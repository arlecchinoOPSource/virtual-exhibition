# Third-party asset register

Every external asset the exhibition considers is listed here, whether it is used or not. The process is in [ASSET_PIPELINE.md](ASSET_PIPELINE.md#third-party-asset-intake). Being on this list proves only that a page exists. An asset counts as imported only once its row has a download date, checksum and measurements and its credit is in `public/models/CREDITS.md`.

**Source audit:** the 33 poly.pizza links and the texture link come from the owner's Canva board. They were read on 2026-11-26 from each page's own text; no model files were downloaded for the audit. Poly.pizza pages show no labelled triangle counts, so none are recorded until a model is imported and measured.

**Licences:**

- **CC0:** Public Domain, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required, but we credit the creator anyway.
- **CC BY 3.0:** Creative Commons Attribution, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). It requires the title, creator, source, licence and a note of changes.

**Status:**

- **Selected:** chosen for import.
- **Candidate:** licence clear, awaiting its room issue.
- **Deferred:** something needs deciding first.
- **Rejected:** not to be published.
